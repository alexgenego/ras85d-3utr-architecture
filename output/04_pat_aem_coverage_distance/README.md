# PAT, AEM, BAN, Coverage and Functional Distance outputs

This directory contains the authoritative final PAT results and null arrays, the final AEM consensus, Coverage Enrichment and Functional Distance outputs, and additional reproducibility files.

`Ras85D_Architecture_Domain_Table_v1_long_reconstructed_rerun.csv` was regenerated during repository recovery from the reconstructed minimal IEL/projection inputs and the preserved authoritative architecture registry. The regenerated consensus was checked against the preserved final consensus and showed no differences.

The two `Ras85D_BAN_*_reconstructed.csv` files are transparent machine-readable reconstructions of the final four-node BAN and its six reported evidence relationships from the authoritative final analysis summary. They are not presented as byte-identical copies of the historical BAN v1 files.

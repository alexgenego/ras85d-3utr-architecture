# Output provenance

This public output set was assembled from the final manuscript-associated Supplementary Data packages and validated recovery outputs.

## Preserved final outputs

Files copied from the archived Supplementary Data S2-S5 packages retain their original filenames and contents.

## Reconstructed outputs

Three files are explicitly marked as reconstructed:

- `04_pat_aem_coverage_distance/Ras85D_Architecture_Domain_Table_v1_long_reconstructed_rerun.csv` — regenerated from the recovered PAT/AEM inputs and authoritative registry; its 34-row consensus was verified against the preserved final consensus with no discrepancies.
- `04_pat_aem_coverage_distance/Ras85D_BAN_final_nodes_reconstructed.csv`
- `04_pat_aem_coverage_distance/Ras85D_BAN_final_edges_reconstructed.csv`

The BAN files are machine-readable reconstructions of the final four-node/six-edge evidence network from the authoritative final analysis summary. The lost historical BAN v1 CSVs are not silently replaced or represented as original files.

## Historical BPU limitation

The complete historical Boundary Pair Universe pair table is not reproduced because the two normalized historical source files (`normalized_architecture.csv` and `interface_catalogue.csv`) were not retained. Preserved final compact boundary-neighborhood outputs and QC records are archived instead.

Quality control

This directory contains quality-control records and validation logs associated with the manuscript analyses.

The QC materials document:

validation of coordinate mappings;
removal of exact duplicate annotations;
exclusion of obsolete or legacy object assignments;
consistency checks between species-specific and alignment-based coordinates;
verification of final object registries;
validation of analysis inputs and outputs;
recording of fixed random seeds and permutation settings where applicable;
decisions made during construction of the final reproducibility archive.

The QC files are intended to document the transition from exploratory working analyses to the final manuscript-associated datasets and scripts.

Only records relevant to the final reported analyses are included. Internal notes, obsolete intermediate files, and redundant working versions are excluded unless they document an important validation decision.
